# Rookie Rapid training pilot

[Operational instructions, recruitment copy and weekly log (German)](rapid-pilot/start.md).

The 0.7.6 release changes the training flow. The effect on play against humans remains a hypothesis. The practice design draws on [retrieval practice](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) and [observational research on chess practice](https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.1106).

- Recruit 40 consenting adults with non-provisional Lichess Rapid ratings between 800 and 1300. Record two baseline weeks. Randomise within starting rating band and game frequency to 0.7.5 or 0.7.6. Run six weeks of training with the same assigned weekly minutes and number of rated 10+0 games. Follow up after two more weeks.
- Ask participants to log minutes in Rookie, playing against humans and other chess study separately. With consent, collect pseudonymised move lists for the first eight eligible games in the baseline window and first eight in the final two intervention weeks. Use anonymous participant codes and publish only aggregates. Report dropouts, missing games and changes in opponent ratings or play frequency by arm.
- Freeze the Stockfish version, depth and error definition before comparing results. Use the rate of major errors per 100 eligible own moves as the primary endpoint. Analyse change per participant, report the between-arm difference with an interval and account for missing outcomes. Secondary measures include game-to-review completion, actual training hours and non-provisional rating change. A separate unaided test in unseen positions is a follow-up. Verify provisional status on Lichess at measurement time. Rookie's imported game history does not establish it.
- Proceed to a larger efficacy study if at least 70% in each arm provide usable game data and the share of weeks with an imported game plus a completed review improves by 15 percentage points, with the game error rate moving in the favourable direction. These are decision thresholds for the pilot. If completion improves but game performance does not, first test short mixed safety exercises and transfer in unseen positions before expanding coaching. Revisit coach fading only after there is sufficient unaided game data.
